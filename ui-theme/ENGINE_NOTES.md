# Engine notes (interface theme)

Findings about how the game and the launcher draw their interface, used by the builders in `tools/`.

## Main menu (Data\topmenu.xml)

- The menu windows (`MAINMENU`, `SOLO`, `MULTI`, `LEADERBOARDS`, `OPTIONS`) are `left="30" top="40" width="548" height="768"`. Their buttons are placed from `POSITIONING x="41"` and the title from `TOPMENUSELECTION` `IFACERECT name="title"` (left 41, width 454).
- The `IFACEBORDER name="border"` of `TOPMENUSELECTION` follows the window width, not the buttons. Narrower buttons alone leave a wide frame, so the windows have to shrink by the same amount.
- Button pieces are drawn at their texture size: the end pieces with `add_smooth`, the middle tiled. The texture height is the button height (`TopMenuSelection::on_button_redraw` 0x59EAC0; hover row at top-38, pressed / disabled at top+38 in the game's atlas).
- `IFACEBORDER` (draw_border 0x51D260): corners are drawn inside the rect at their own texture size, edges are tiled. corner0..3 = TL, TR, BR, BL; edge0..3 = top, right, bottom, left; extra0..7 are optional ornaments (`TEX_NONE` hides them).
- The logo (`<LOGO>`) is drawn lit: diffuse `ron_logo_texture_map.tga` with normal map `ron_logo_normal_map.tga`; the specular pass uses `ronlogo_specmap.tga` (uniform white, low alpha). Rows above ~60 of the 512x512 texture are off screen; rows below ~405 run into the title plate.
- Background: `ANIM` blocks play in turn (30 s each). An `ANIMLAYER` is drawn at a size proportional to its `IFACETEX` rect, made for 1280x720: a bigger texture shows magnified, not sharper.

## Dialogs (Data\interface.xml)

- `MESSAGEBOX name="messagebox"` (450x150) is a yes / no question box (not the main menu's quit question, see POPUP below). It has the `large_button_*` pieces (its buttons), `button_border_*` (a dark band behind the buttons, from wrapper_pieces_01 at 229..511, 436) and `IFACEBORDER name="misc_border"` (the wooden frame with 42 px corners).
- Texture ids (`IFACETEXLOAD id=...`) are declared per window block, so a new atlas must be loaded inside each window that uses it.
- Exception, `POPUP`: an `IFACETEXLOAD` inside a `POPUPSTYLE` works in the menus but not in a game, where the in-game pop-ups ("Do you want to resign this game?") drew the pieces as plain white quads (texture index 0). The original popup buttons use `TEX_DIPLO`, which is loaded in the global list at the top of interface.xml (`IFACERENDERMANAGER`); `TEX_REVIVED_MENU` is declared there too now (not yet confirmed in game).
- The end of game window (Victory / Defeat) is `ENDGAMEWIN` (`ENDGAMEWINRANKED` in ranked games), with the same `butnormal0..2` / `buthighlight0..2` / `butdown0..2` from `TEX_DIPLO` (12/149/12 x 32) as the in-game menu (`MAINMENU`); its four buttons are 250x32.
- The main menu's "Are you sure you want to quit?" is a `POPUP` (`POPUPSTYLE name="default"`), not the `MESSAGEBOX`. Its buttons are `butnormal0..2` / `buthighlight0..2` / `butdown0..2` from `TEX_DIPLO` (art\iface_diplomacy_01.tga, 336..511 x 157..255); its background is `IFACECOLOR bg_color`. The same piece names appear in 9 other windows (diplomacy), so edits must stay inside `<POPUP>`.

## Game setup screen (Data\setupwin.xml)

- Child windows are clipped to their parent: `Window::recurse_clips` 0x00A50070 intersects each child's rect with the parent's clip. A control placed past its panel's edge is cut off (and cannot be clicked there).
- Start / Back are `BUTTON name="start"` / `"cancel"` inside `PLAYERSWINSOLO` (and MP / GameSpy / ranked). `SetupWin::start_button_disable` 0x005AE570 and `setup_buttons` 0x005C1A30 find them by name in the players panel (`SetupWin+0x25D4`, vtable +0x424; a miss is skipped). Moving them to the `SETUPWIN` root would leave Start never disabled.
- `SetupWin::on_button_redraw` 0x005C28E0 draws the `large_button_*` pieces with `IFaceRenderManager::draw_expander` 0x0051C450: end pieces at their texture width, the middle tiled, and all three **stretched to the button's height**. Taller buttons need no new texture.
- A `BUTTON` takes a `<FONT size="" style=""/>` child (the game's own lobby tabs use it).
- The `STRETCHSPRITE name="misc"` (the `button_border_*` oval, 75 px texture) is stretched to its `IFACERECT`; its recess is rows 12..49 of the 75 and starts 13 px from the left end, 18 px from the right end (the right piece carries the shadow).
- The screen frame's corner brackets (anim_overlay) reach down to 5% of the screen height; the panels start at 6.5%.
- Taller rows (2026-11-05): `on_combo_redraw` draws `combo_left`, `combo_right` and `combo_arrow` at their **texture height** and tiles `combo_mid` at its texture height, whatever the combo's rect. A taller combo needs taller pieces, and since the pieces are declared once in the `SETUPWIN` block, every combo of the screen must have their height. Since then they come from `art\revived\setup_combo.tga` (`TEX_REVIVED_COMBO`, build_reskin.py `tall_combo_atlas`), 26 px, and every COMBOBOX / player rect in setupwin.xml is 26 high.
- The rules panel's major rules are not placed by their `POSITIONING`: they follow `first_major_rule` / `second_major_rule` (top of the first row, the step = second top - first top, the width = `percent_width -600`, 60% of the panel), seen on a screenshot (rows 26 apart at 47 / 73). The minor rules (Start With...) follow `first_minor_rule` / `second_minor_rule` of `CUSTOMWIN`, which lies inside the rules panel (`top` counted from the panel's top). Only the shown rules are laid out, one after the other; 13 fit between the two dividers.
- Single player rows: each row's inner `POSITIONING y` is added to the outer `y + inc_y * row`; rows 1..7 have `y="37"` (was 30) to leave the "Other Nations" combo (`rules3`) its line under row 0.
- Fonts: `SetupWin::exec` loads three fonts with `FontFamily::get(12 / 14 / 16, bold)` into `SetupWin+0x2268 / +0x226C / +0x2270`; `setup_combos` gives the combos of `CUSTOMWIN` (`SetupWin+0x2F04`) the 12 one and every other combo the 14 one (Buffer font `+0x1D0`), the check boxes get the 12 one. The fonts that exist are the ones `Data\fonts.xml` lists per `RESOLUTION` (1280 and 1024): 12 bold is Arial, 14 and 18 bold Georgia; a size not listed gives the closest one. This theme's `fonts.xml` (tools\build_fonts.py) adds Arial 15 bold and Georgia 20 bold; camera-smooth's flags.c asks for 15 / 18 / 20 (`setup_font_percent`).
- `Buffer::write_l` centres the text vertically in its rect (`(height - text_height) / 2`), so taller rows need no other change for the text.

## Shared atlases

- `wrapper_pieces_01.tga`: the 294x294 wooden frame at 1,1 is the `IFACEBORDER` set used by most windows (corners 42x42 at 1,1 / 252,1 / 252,252 / 1,252; extras are the bar segments between them); `edge0` 1,338 and `edge2` 1,295 (295x42), `edge1` 295,1 and `edge3` 339,1 (41x262). The visible bar sits at the inner side of the 42 px band (outer ~17 px is shadow). `small_bar` 45,44 205x11, `large_bar` 295,264 190x13, `button_border_*` 229..511 x 436..511 (the oval around Start / Back).
- Drop-down fields: `SetupWin::on_combo_redraw` 0x005C47C0 draws `combo_left` and `combo_right` at their texture size (`add_smooth`) and **tiles** `combo_mid` across the rest (`IFaceRenderManager::tile_horiz` 0x0051F1D0 at 0x005C4DB6), so a texture on the field must repeat seamlessly every 130 px. Each 21 px piece: light rim (row top), dark line (top+1), field (top+2..top+18), dark line (top+19), rim (top+20); left piece x 193 rim, 194 line, 195..196 field; right piece 329..330 field, 331 line, 332 rim. The open list's rows (`SetupWin::on_pulldown_item_redraw` 0x005C43F0) use `draw_expander` 0x0051C450 with WHITE / GREY colour on the same kind of pieces. build_reskin.py `grain_fields` paints the field grey with a grain.
- `wrapper_pieces_02.tga`: check boxes (1,1 / 1,70), drop-down field (193..333 x 1 / 24), arrow (123,1), `large_button` (193,47 17/142/17x30), `small_button` (400,0), tabs (1,139 down / 1,256 up, 12/85/12x38), the yellow "selected" pills at 245..395 x 140..200.
- `Wrapper_pieces_gamespy.tga`: lobby pieces in 205..505 x 0..305; the rest are pictures (globes, hand, logos).
- Replacing these files in mod_override reskins every window that uses them without touching their XML.

- **Magenta gutters (2026-11-08):** `art\iface_AS0_assets.tga` (TEX_CIV, 512 x 512, 32-bit, uncompressed) keeps its pieces apart with 1-pixel lines of opaque magenta (255, 0, 255; 10763 pixels). The interface is drawn enlarged and filtered, so a piece's edge picks up the gutter: pink lines at every joint of the tiled frieze `bar` (390, 131, 122 x 34; the column at x 389 is magenta) and on the edges of `building_decoration2`, `help_button`, `thin_vert_bar`, whose rects reach a pixel into a gutter. No piece uses magenta on purpose. `tools/fix_iface_gutters.py` gives every magenta pixel its non-magenta neighbours' colour (opaque ones first) and writes `mod_override/art/iface_AS0_assets.tga` with the game's own header and orientation.

## Command button icons (Data\options.xml)

- Every command button's icon comes from `options.xml`: the n-th `<OPTION>` is option code n (`OptionIndex`), with `tex_id` / `tex_col` / `tex_row` giving its cell. `TEX_ORDERS_0` is `art\iface_unit_orders.tga`, cells 62x40 (61x39 drawn). Found 2026-10-27.
- Air codes: 0x24 Air Mission (5,8; selected aircraft, slot 0,0, from `UnitOut::make_options`), 0x2B Scramble (7,7), 0x2C Repeat Orders (0,11), 0x2D Return To Base (2,1), 0x2E Recall (0,10). Attack is 0x0A (0,5). The Airbase itself gets only Scramble, Repeat and Recall (`BuildOut::make_options` 0x0062B364); its strike is a right-click with the Airbase selected, there is no button. camera-smooth's airstrike.c adds 0x24 to the Airbase.
- The theme overrides `options.xml` with Air Mission set to the Attack cell (0,5). Only the English file (`options.xml`; `.4`, `.7` ... are other languages) is overridden.

## Launcher (patriots.exe)

- Native MFC dialog (timestamp 597669ED) that loads this mod's winmm.dll too. Window: `launcher_res\background.png` (online) / `background_offline.png`, 752x732 with alpha, shown through `UpdateLayeredWindow`.
- Buttons are owner-drawn from bitmap resources: `BUTTONU` / `BUTTOND` (191x34, normal / pressed, no hover), `IDB_CLOSE_U` (26x24), `IDB_MINIMIZE_U` (25x24), loaded through MFC with `user32!LoadBitmapW`. Captions are drawn with GDI+ (`GdipCreateFontFamilyFromName` "Palatino Linotype", `GdipDrawString`).
- Dialog 102 (480x418 dialog units, MS Shell Dlg 8): Play / Achievements / Website / Workshop at x 12, y 147 / 181 / 215 / 249, 127x21 DLU; close 433,18; minimize 413,18; a WebBrowser control (1001) at 170,34 280x370. In background pixels: x * 1.5 + 17, y * 1.625 + 27.
- The launcher is **two windows**. Dialog 103 (empty, 320x200 DLU) is the background: the drawing function (RVA 0x12B0..0x1495) loads the PNG with `GdipCreateBitmapFromFile` (background_offline.png when byte [this+0x8B0] is 0), makes an HBITMAP, reads its size with `GetObject` and passes it to `UpdateLayeredWindow`, so the window takes the PNG's size by itself. Dialog 102 (720x679 px, WS_POPUP, no frame) holds the controls and is owned by 103, which centres it with MFC's CenterWindow: offset = bg/2 - dialog/2 with each half rounded down, (16,27) at 752x732, (18,31) at 865x842. Control positions in the background are therefore client + that offset (Play's client x is 18, so 34 in the background).
- Dialog 102 is see-through by colour key: `SetLayeredWindowAttributes(magenta, 0x96, LWA_COLORKEY)` at RVA 0x2DF6, and its background is bitmap resource 154 (752x732, all magenta) set by `CDialogEx::SetBackgroundImage(154, BACKGR_TOPLEFT, TRUE)` at RVA 0x2DDE. A dialog larger than 752x732 shows the plain grey dialog colour past the bitmap; launcher.c patches the location to BACKGR_TILE (byte at RVA 0x2DE1, 1 -> 0) so magenta fills any size.
- Scaling the launcher (layout.ini `[window] scale_percent`, 115 since 2026-10-26): the builder draws the PNG and bitmaps that much larger, and launcher.c grows dialog 102 by the scale and puts each control at the scaled place of the background it sat on. Moving 102 by itself (for example to centre it on the screen) detaches it from the background; 103 is already centred on the screen by the launcher.
- patriots.exe does not import winmm.dll itself (no "winmm" string in it). Started through Steam, the Steam overlay (which imports it) loads the mod's winmm.dll from the game folder; started directly (double-click on patriots.exe), only system DLLs load winmm, from System32, so the mod and its theme do not load at all (2026-10-26: the windows kept 752x732 / 720x679). Test the launcher through `steam://rungameid/287450`.
- The background window gets its picture only through `UpdateLayeredWindow`, imported by name by patriots.exe: launcher.c patches that import and gives every call for the window its own picture (same size), which is how the panel's pictures change (layout.ini `[slides]`, a `SetTimer` on the background window, which the launcher's message loop serves). The controls' dialog lies over it, see-through by colour key, so a control's bitmap over the panel needs magenta wherever the picture should show (minimize and close).
- `GdipBitmapLockBits` with PixelFormat32bppPARGB (0x000E200B) gives premultiplied pixels as `UpdateLayeredWindow` (AC_SRC_ALPHA) wants; data held in alpha (the fog's order) must be read as 32bppARGB (0x0026200A) or the colour is multiplied by it.
- Captions (2026-10-29): the launcher reads them from `launcher_res\locale.ini` only (format string `%s\launcher_res\locale.ini`), `[launcher]` keys 170023 Play, 170024 Workshop, 170025 RoN Website, 170026 Leaderboards (not shown), 170027 Achievements, 170032-170037 the Workshop agreement (text, checkbox, "Agreement:", Accept, Decline), through `GetPrivateProfileStringA/W` (imports of the exe). Built-in defaults when a key is missing: "Play", "Workshop", "RoN Website", "Achievement". The per-language folders `launcher_res\br de en es fr it jp ko nl ru zh\locale.ini` are never read by the exe (the root copy here is the `en` one); their texts are partly cut ("Spl", "Akzep") or stale (`br`, `ru`, `nl` map 170024 to "Coming Soon"). ANSI files (cp1252, `ru` cp1251). The captions go out through `GdipDrawString` (exe import) on each owner-drawn button's redraw, so a redraw shows a changed caption.
- The game's language is `Language=<code>` in `%APPDATA%\Microsoft Games\Rise of Nations\rise2.ini`, section `[RISE OF NATIONS]` (also the windowed size); the launcher has no language setting of its own. `launcher_lang.c` writes that key and redirects the locale.ini reads to `mod_override\launcher_res\locale\<code>.ini` (UTF-16 with BOM, so `GetPrivateProfileStringW` keeps Cyrillic etc.).
- Its button is an owned popup (`WS_EX_LAYERED | WS_EX_NOACTIVATE`, `UpdateLayeredWindow` with per-pixel alpha) over dialog 102: a layered child would need a Windows 8 manifest, and the dialog's colour key would fringe anti-aliased edges. Mouse clicks pass through the popup's alpha-0 pixels, so it may overlap Workshop. It follows the dialog through a subclass (`WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED`, `WM_SHOWWINDOW`).
- Close (1008) and minimize (1013) keep their template size (24x24, 23x23) after their bitmaps load: CBitmapButton does not size them to the bitmap, so the bitmaps must have the button's size.
