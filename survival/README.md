# Survival

A game mode: hold out for 45 minutes against waves of enemies that march in from the edges of the map. You start in the middle of an open plain; the enemy has no cities, only armies, and they are one or two ages behind you.

Version 3, 2026-11-10: the Survival button in the Solo menu, the Survival map (flat land, no water, you in the middle), waves from the four edges, the no-build band along the edges, the wave bar at the top of the screen. Parts:

- the game script `scenario/Scripts/Survival/survival.bhs` (the waves, the band, the victory);
- the map style `mapstyles/SURVIVAL.xml` (woods, rocks and resources of the Survival map), installed into the game's `mapstyles\`;
- the support in the mod's `winmm.dll`: `camera-smooth/src/survival.c` (the button, the setup's fixed Map Style and Script, the map, the bar, the clocks, the band; `survival_support` and `survival_map` under `[gameplay]`, `survival_bar`, `survival_bar_top`, `survival_bar_length`, `survival_clock_y` under `[ui]` in `ron_camera.ini`);
- the button itself and the bar's pictures in the interface theme: `ui-theme/tools/build_menu_theme.py` (the button) and `ui-theme/tools/build_survival_bar.py` (`art\revived\survival_bar.tga`).

Without the DLL the script still runs on any map chosen under Script, with no bar, no clocks and no band.

## How to play

1. **Solo** menu > **Survival** (under Quick Battle). The Quick Battle setup opens.
2. Your nation, the starting age, the map size and your Difficulty (it is the battle director's: it sets the waves). The setup shows only your row: Other Nations is set to 1, the battle director, and its rows are hidden (`survival_setup_checks`, from nonukes.c's setup_checks hook). A smaller map brings the waves to you sooner. Every rule of the setup is fixed and greyed (`k_rules` in survival.c: Big Huge map size, Normal speed, Small Town, Standard resources, Normal technology, population 200, Ancient to Information, Elimination Conquest, Victory Conquest shown as "Survival"; Map Style reads "Survival plain" and Script "Survival"); Turbo Resources, No Nation Powers, No Unique Units, Save to Profile, the Nukes slider and the Units button are hidden, no unit bans apply (flags.c's combo text hook asks `survival_combo_text`).
3. The computer player's **difficulty** sets the waves:

| Difficulty | Enemy age | Wave size |
|---|---|---|
| Easiest, Easy, Moderate | 2 ages behind you | smaller |
| Hard, Tough, Toughest | 1 age behind you | bigger |

Picking **Survival** under **Script** in a custom game does the same (also in multiplayer: every player needs the mod). The map style chosen in the setup does not matter: a Survival game always gets the Survival map.

## What happens

- **The map:** all land, flat, no water, no rivers, no mountains or cliffs; dry steppe or savannah ground, light woods and rocks, none near the edges. The human player starts in the middle; the computer players get starts on a ring three eighths of the map from the middle (and lose them at once, below).
- At the start the computer player's capital, Citizens and Scout are removed (the first 10 seconds). It is not defeated: it only sends waves. Any other computer player at war with you is removed the same way (and is defeated); computer allies stay.
- **Waves:** the first at 7:00 (seven minutes to set up), then every 80 seconds, one second less per minute played, at least 45 seconds, times `WAVE_SPACING`. Each wave is bigger: about 4 + 1.5 per wave soldiers (times the difficulty), then times `WAVE_PCT` (18 %, the user, 2026-11-10: the waves were massive; a third, then 35 % less, then 15 % less), at least 2, at most 60 x 18 % a wave and 200 alive. Boss waves the same; siege 1 + one per 24 waves in every other wave from wave 6 (normal), 1 + one per 20 minutes in every other boss wave (the user: half the siege).
- **Goal and final assault** (2026-11-11): the Survival setup's three goal buttons (`goal_wonder`, `goal_tech`, `goal_time` in setupwin.xml, the game's alert pictures `wonder_notice`, `final_science_notice`, `hour_glass_large` from `art\iface_alerts.tga`; `[survival] goal` in ron_camera.ini). The script asks with the timers `surv_goal_wonder` / `surv_goal_tech` (the DLL makes the chosen one expire at once; neither in multiplayer: the time). The final assault comes when a defender has Wonder points (goal 0), reaches the Information Age (goal 1), or at `SURVIVE_SECONDS` (always): the wave block's size times 3, four sides, a General in each part, siege in each; then no more waves, the clocks and the bar go (`surv_end` set to 1 second). Every 5 seconds (`fincheck`) the script checks: the wave player has no military units left: victory; with goal 0, the Wonder's owner has no Wonder points left: defeat.
- **Length** (2026-11-11): the Survival setup's Time Limit combo (id 0x48, Game+0x3C, rules.xml `timelimits`), shown in that setup with items 1-4 only (30, 45, 60, 90 minutes), named Short / Medium / Long / Very long; `[survival] length` in ron_camera.ini (1-4). The script asks with `surv_len_30` / `surv_len_60` / `surv_len_90` (none: 45, also without the Survival button) and sets `SURVIVE_SECONDS`; `age_pace` grows with it (times minutes / 45), so the waves reach the same ages by the end.
- **Reward cards** (2026-11-11; camera-smooth `cards.c`): when a boss wave's General falls (the script keeps its id, `bossg`; or 4 minutes after it came, `bossdone`) the script sends `surv_bosscards P n` for every defender: the local player picks one of three in a panel in the middle of the screen, the game paused; a computer ally takes one at random. 23 kinds (cards.c's `k_cards`): buildings, hero, army, at once; bonuses in quarters (a boss card 4, a small card 1), capped (`card_cap[]` here, the same in cards.c). A pick goes back to the script as a 9-bit code (player, kind, size) through `surv_pick`, `surv_pkb 0..8` and `surv_pkack`; the script does Tribute (`give_good`, 180 x (age + 1) of Food, Timber, Wealth and Metal), Mercenaries (two squads of `li[]` / `hi[]` at the capital) and Respite (the next normal wave smaller), and sends the counts back (`surv_cards P q0 .. q22`, also every minute, so a saved game keeps them).
- **Ruins** (2026-11-11): every 3 seconds (`ruins`) the script looks for enemy buildings it keeps (`bid[]`, the camps, the Towers and Forts and the new ones, up to `NBMAX` 160) that have fallen and, one time in two (`RUIN_PCT` 50), sends `surv_ruin x y`: cards.c lays a Goody Box there (the map's ruins). Exploring one gives the game's resources and a small card (`Unit::explore_goody`, hooked).
- **Expansion** (2026-11-11): every `EXPAND_SECONDS` (120) a new building next to one still standing (8 to 14 tiles, inside the band, at least 22 % of the map from the middle, `territory_owner` not a defender): a camp (two times in three; a spawn point like the others) or a Tower.
- **Spacing:** `WAVE_SPACING` (3, the user, 2026-11-10: "de 3 ori mai rar dar sa fie de 3 ori mai numeroase") multiplies the interval and the size; the wave number counts as that many waves for the growth and the sides; `BOSS_EVERY` 2. The script sends it as the fifth number of `surv_plan`, `wave_times` in survival.c multiplies its steps by it.
- **Hero squad size:** the hero is created as one squad bigger than its type's: the script sets `surv_uber P size name` just before its `create_unit` and `surv_uber P 0 name` right after; survival.c sets the type's UBER_SIZE (+0x308, and the nation's own version's, `get_graft`) for that moment, since `Objects::init_unit` 0x0065E0C0 reads it when it makes the squad. Six infantry, two riders. Refills (ranks.c `c_hero_uber`): the infantry to twice its type's size times the hero power (6, 8, 12, 14, 18), the riders to 2. The DLL slots are 4 * (player - 1) + kind (+ 2 for a second half, unused now; `hero_pair_sync` would keep two halves at one experience).
- **Computer allies' heroes:** every computer ally gets the rider (one unit, so its id stays: the script can follow it). Every 5 seconds (`allycare`) the script sends it back to its capital when it is below 60 % of its hit points or more than 20 tiles away; otherwise its AI uses it. Mix: 40 % light infantry, 25 % heavy infantry, 15 % archers / mounted ranged / machine guns, 20 % cavalry; siege from wave 6.
- **Co-op:** the battle director is the computer player at war with the first human player; everyone else not at war with that human defends (other humans, computer allies; other enemy computers are removed at the start). Every human player gets a hero (the choice of the Survival setup's buttons in a single-player game, the infantry squad in multiplayer, where no setup button exists), in the DLL's hero slot 2 * (player - 1) + kind. The waves grow by 60 % for every defender past the first (size, the 60 cap and the 200 alive alike), come in at least one part per defender (up to four), and each part attack-moves on a defender picked at random. The DLL gives the other humans and the computers on the first human's team (Game's player slots, the team byte) starts round the middle, a tenth of the map from it. Without teams (Survival of the Fittest, the Survival setup's) every computer player but the battle director (the last) is made the humans' and the others' ally (`make_alliance` both ways, at the start); in a team game a computer allied with the director stays an enemy and is removed. `coop_test=1` under `[survival]` keeps the computer rows and Other Nations in the Survival setup; Other Nations counts the allies only (1 to begin with) and survival.c adds the director after them when Start is pressed (`survival_before_game`, from nonukes.c's setup_game hook). The director, the last computer row, is set to white (colour 6).
- **Enemy buildings:** 12 seconds into the game the script cuts the map inside the no-build band into a `GRID` x `GRID` (7) grid and builds one Barracks, Stable or Siege Factory (in turn, neighbours differ) at a random point in the middle of every cell whose middle is at least 22 % of the map from the map's middle: 36 on a Big Huge map. A Tower stands on every inner corner of the grid at least 30 % out (12) and a Fort on each diagonal, 36 % out (4); those are not spawn points.
- **Where:** from one of those buildings still standing, picked at random for every part of a wave: infantry and the General out of the nearest Barracks, cavalry out of the nearest Stable, siege out of the nearest Siege Factory (4 tiles towards the middle), out of the picked one when none of a kind is left. When all of them are destroyed (a notice says so), at the edge of the map (two tiles in), on a land tile away from the corners and off screen. From one camp or side for waves 1 to 3, two sides from wave 4, three from wave 10, all four from wave 18, the soldiers split between them. Each part attack-moves to the capital (or any building) of one of the defenders.
- **No-build band:** a tenth of the map along all four edges (at least 6 tiles), kept free so the waves always come in. Nobody can build there ("Can't build in enemy territory."), the computer players neither. Flags mark its inner edge (single player only).
- **Wave bar** standing at the right edge of the screen, under the clocks (from the DLL; the user wanted it vertical, there is more room there): a circle per wave at its time down the 45 minutes (smaller when there are many); grey once it has come, the next one amber with a pulsing glow, the future ones dark; the boss waves larger, red, with a skull; a white pointer at the present, left of the bar; "Wave 7 / 27" under it. It reaches down to 84 % of the screen's height: the script hides the players' score list (`ui_hide_score()` at the start and every 15 seconds).
- **Clocks** at the top right (from the DLL): time left to survive, and time to the next wave (yellow when it is a boss wave, red in the last 10 seconds).
- **Age:** the waves use the generic unit of each line in an age of their own, by the wave number (the user, 2026-11-11: not behind the defenders): wave 1 Ancient, then one age every `age_pace` / 100 waves, 240 on Easiest, 225, 210, 195, 180, 165 on Toughest (Enlightenment on Easiest, Modern on Toughest by the last waves; 180 .. 130 was too fast); the final assault one age more. Your own ageing no longer changes them.
- **Boss waves** 30 seconds after every fifth wave (after waves 5, 10, 15 ...), besides the normal waves, from one side: a General, heavy cavalry (tanks later), siege and heavy infantry, more each time. None when the fifth wave comes in the last minute.
- Idle wave units are sent at a defender again every 15 seconds.
- The wave player takes no attrition (otherwise your territory, which can cover most of the map, would melt every wave on the way).
- **The hero:** at the start the script creates a squad of the first infantry line (`li[]`, the Barracks' leftmost: Slingers .. Assault Infantry, the nation's own version) three tiles from your capital and gives its id to the DLL (`surv_hero P id`). ranks.c lets it rank to level 30 (its 12 experience bits count 8 at a time; levels 11-30 +2 % damage, +3 % hit points, +2 % attack rate each, insignia of level 10, rank name "Hero"). Every 15 seconds the script names the line's unit of your age (`surv_heroage P name`); survival.c turns the hero's soldiers into it (the unit's set_type, as a researched upgrade does; the rank stays). It does not come back when it falls. Not yet: the extra soldiers at levels 15, 20, 25 and 30 (the game refills a squad up to its type's UBER_SIZE, `Unit::repair_damage`; a hero-only maximum needs a hook there), and the hero after loading a saved game whose first captain has fallen.
- **Victory:** every defender still standing at 45:00 wins. Defeat: the game's normal rules (losing all your cities).

## Settings (top of the script)

`SURVIVE_SECONDS` (2700, 45 minutes), `FIRST_WAVE` (240), `MAX_ALIVE` (200), `BOSS_EVERY` (5: a boss after every fifth wave), `BOSS_DELAY` (30 seconds after it), `debug_lines` (1 = a chat line per wave and side: where, which age, for whom). The unit tables by age are the arrays `li`, `hi`, `rg`, `lc`, `hc`, `sg`. The bar follows `SURVIVE_SECONDS`, `FIRST_WAVE`, `BOSS_EVERY` and `BOSS_DELAY` (the script sends them as `surv_plan`); a change to the interval rule must be made in `survival.c`'s `wave_times` too.

## Not yet

- The setup screen keeps Quick Battle's other choices: more than one computer player can be set (the extra enemies are removed at the start), and Teams & Allies is not fixed.
