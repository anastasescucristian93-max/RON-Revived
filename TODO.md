## Balance (for later)

- 2026-10-26, Romania's balance (asked by the user, "average of all nations as the reference"): `tools/nation_strength.py <unitrules.xml> [nation]` scores each nation's unit per military line and age (power = sqrt(attack / recharge x hits), efficiency = power / cost) against the average of the 25 nations. By unit stats Romania (generic units) is exactly average in every age (0.99-1.00, rank 10-13 of 25). Between all nations the unit stats differ little (+-3 % per age); the standouts: Japanese Samurai 1.50, Inca Inti Clubmen / Macemen 1.14-1.17, British Longbowmen efficiency 1.20, Russian Cossacks efficiency 1.18, T-80 efficiency 1.16; Spanish Tercios 0.80, Persian Immortals efficiency 0.77-0.85 (they have other strengths). Many uniques have the generic stats (Legions = Phalanx): their nations' strength is in the powers (cost, time, free units), which the script leaves out. Next: put numbers on the powers; decide Romania's unique units.

Analysis of 2026-10-24, from the game's own files: nation powers in `Data/rules.xml` (constants such as `BANTU_POP_CAP`) and `Data/help.xml` (`TRIBE0`..`TRIBE23`), unique units in `Data/unitrules.xml` (each unique unit names the generic unit it replaces in `<GRAFT>`; `<TRIBE_MASK>` is read from the right, bit 0 = Aztecs, in `help.xml` order). Score used: attack / recharge x hit points, unique vs generic. It leaves out range, speed, armor, the engine's hidden bonuses (camels vs cavalry and so on) and the map, so it is a first pass only. Script: `tools/uniques.py <game>/Data/unitrules.xml`.

- Look too strong:
  - **Japanese:** Samurai recharge 15 against 32 for Elite Pikemen, 2.3 times the damage per hit point. The whole spear line has +1 armor and a slightly faster attack (+16-20 %). Barracks units are 7 % cheaper and 10 % faster to make for every age (-35 % and -50 % by age 5).
  - **Bantu:** population limit 250 against 200 (+25 % army at the cap), +1 city, cities 75 % cheaper, units and citizens 25 % faster. Light infantry 17 % faster to make and 8-10 % stronger, upgrades without military research.
  - **Chinese:** citizens created instantly and Large Cities from the start. Manchu units 18 % faster to make with +1-2 range. Heavy Fire Lances +19 %.
  - **British:** Longbowmen +2 range (12 against 10), +17 % power and cheaper, so +31-33 % power per resource. Free archers with every Barracks.
  - **Russians:** Cossack line attacks 14 % faster, costs 10 % less and is made 11 % faster, about +26 % value per resource.
  - **Inca:** Inti Clubmen and Macemen +32-39 % power, but melee in place of ranged units. 25 % refund on lost units.
- Look too weak:
  - **Persians:** the Immortals line is 9-12 % weaker than the pikemen it replaces, and two of them cost more (0.73-0.80 power per resource). It throws javelins at range 6, which the score does not count. The powers are modest (+50 % food only at the start, civics -30 %).
  - **Spanish:** Tercios attack 37 % less often (recharge 44 against 32) in exchange for range 5: 0.65-0.73. The power depends on the map (ruins, water).
    - The user, 2026-10-26: the Spanish feel underpowered in play and need improvements; first nation to rebalance. To propose: Tercios with a shorter recharge (closer to the 32 of the generic line) or more hit points, and a look at the nation power (ruins, water) so it pays off on land maps too.
  - **Americans:** units only +6 % hit points; the bonuses come late (free bombers from the Modern Age).
  - **Maya, Nubians, Egyptians, Lakota, Greeks, Romans, Iroquois:** unique units have the same stats as the generic ones (some only move faster: Greek cavalry +20 %, Romans +8 %, Lakota +10 %). All their strength is in the nation power.
